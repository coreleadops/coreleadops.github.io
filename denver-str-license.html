What Denver requires of hosts

Summary of the city's published requirements. Always confirm current rules with the City and County of Denver Excise and Licenses before you apply.

  • Primary residence only. Short-term rentals are allowed only at the host's full-time primary residence. Investment properties cannot be licensed.
  • License before you list. Applications can take roughly 30–45 days to process.
  • License number on every listing. It must appear in each advertisement.
  • Liability insurance. At least $1 million covering short-term rental use, unless the hosting platform provides it.
  • Local responder. Hosts who aren't on-site must name a representative who can respond to problems within 60 minutes.
  • Manager changes. Changes to a general manager's contact details must be filed within 30 days.

Requirements as published by the city and summarized by third-party guides in 2026. Rules change; we re-check before every filing.

Questions

Can I get a license for an investment property?

Under Denver's published rules, short-term rentals are limited to the host's primary residence, so investment properties generally can't be licensed. We'll tell you up front if your situation doesn't qualify, before you pay for an application.

Do you submit the application for me?

We prepare and review everything, then you submit under your own account with our walkthrough, since the license is in your name.

Are the city fees included?

No. City fees are paid directly to the city and are separate from our flat fee.
